01 / Generative AI · Data access

Natural-language access to scientific data

Designed an NL2SQL system for scientists who need answers across complex databases without writing SQL. The workflow grounds requests in schema metadata and domain concepts, then checks generated queries before execution.

NL2SQLMetadata retrievalSQL guardrails
60% faster data retrieval turnaround · >47% direct execution accuracy on zero-shot domain queries
Read the approach

Problem. Finding the right tables, fields, and joins across scientific data systems took specialist support.

Decision. I separated intent understanding, schema selection, SQL generation, and safe execution. Domain metadata and relationships supplied the context; validation and clarification handled unsafe or ambiguous requests.

Outcome. The system was designed for 1,000+ scientists and achieved a 3.5× lift in direct execution accuracy over baseline prompting in the measured zero-shot evaluation.

02 / Document AI

Clinical data extraction at scale

Modernized extraction of pharmacokinetic parameters from long, inconsistent clinical study reports using document structure, targeted retrieval, and an internal LLM service.

Document AILLMsInformation extraction
800+ reports · >65% faster processing · 96% extraction accuracy
Read the approach

Problem. Manually locating and transcribing PK summary tables was slow and difficult to scale.

Decision. The workflow used document structure to narrow where to look, then extracted and checked fields such as Cmax and AUC with AbbVie's internal LLM API.

Outcome. It created a reusable route from complex reports to structured data for downstream analysis.

04 / Computer vision

Reducing manual video review

Initiated a two-stage approach to Rat Grimace Scoring: select usable facial frames from study video, then support expert assessment using annotated eyes, ears, nose, and whiskers.

YOLOv8VideoExpert validation
67% less manual video review time for usable frames
Read the approach

Problem. Scientists spent substantial time finding clear facial frames before scoring could begin.

Decision. The first model selected frames suitable for review. A separate scoring workflow used expert labels and facial feature annotations, keeping scientists involved in validation.

Outcome. Frame selection reduced the manual search effort while preserving expert judgment in the scoring process.

Knowledge graphsEntity resolutionRAG

05 / Data engineering · Cloud

A searchable archive for structural biology

Migrated historical X-ray crystallography experiments from on-premises storage to Amazon S3, joining archived files to metadata so scientists could retrieve past work.

AirflowFastAPIAmazon S3
20,000 experiment folders · 57% lower storage costs
Read the approach

Problem. An expensive legacy store held experiments across three historical folder conventions, with limited ways to find archived work.

Decision. Airflow orchestrated discovery and transfer, idempotency checks prevented duplicates, and a FastAPI service linked S3 locations to scientific metadata.

Outcome. Average transfer cycles fell from about 15 to 7–8 minutes while archived experiments became easier to locate.
